About CRDC

Residents organising for a safer settlement

The Community Resilience Disaster Committee (CRDC) was formed by residents of 8ste Laan to reduce disaster risk, improve the local environment, support community development, and expand digital access for the settlement. The committee works closely with Ward 31, Subcouncil 15 and the City of Cape Town's Disaster Management team to raise and resolve issues that affect residents' safety day to day.

CRDC's work spans practical, on-the-ground projects — from anti-dumping and fire-safety messaging to documenting drainage and flood hazards — alongside the longer-term work of formalising the committee as a registered non-profit organisation, so it can access funding and formally employ community members through programmes like the Expanded Public Works Programme (EPWP).

FILE 02 🌧️ Active hazard

8ste Laan Drainage & Road Safety

8ste Laan stormwater channel

Road and drainage works were left incomplete after the contractor, Nejeni Construction, paused works during an unresolved payment dispute with the City. Loose road-base material that was never properly compacted is now washing into the stormwater drainage channel during rain, blocking it — leaving the area worse off for flooding than before construction began. CRDC is documenting the hazard through rain-event walkabouts, with photos, GPS points and timestamps, to press for a resolution.

CauseUncompacted aggregate
RiskWorsened flooding
ActionDocumentation underway
